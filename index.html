How opt-in works

The participant sends the first message.

  1. A participant encounters a Checkpoint sticker, card, or object in person.
  2. The attached card or signage identifies the program as Checkpoint by Secret Soup Company and displays the SMS disclosure before any text is sent.
  3. The participant taps the sticker or object, which opens a prefilled SMS in the participant's own messaging app.
  4. The participant chooses whether to press Send.
  5. No messages are sent to the participant unless the participant sends that first text.

What participants receive

Project messages stay specific to Checkpoint.

  • Conversational replies triggered by the participant's texts.
  • Occasional continuation or check-in messages related to the project.
  • Message frequency varies by participation.
  • Message and data rates may apply depending on the participant's carrier plan.

Controls and support

Participants can stop or ask for help at any time.

  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe.
  • Reply HELP at any time for support information.
  • Email t@trysoup.xyz with questions, problems, or feedback.

Frequently asked questions

Plain answers

Do I have to send the first text myself?

Yes. Tapping a Checkpoint object or sticker only opens a prefilled message. The participant must choose to press Send from their own device.

Is this a marketing list?

No. Checkpoint is a project-specific messaging program operated by Secret Soup Company. It is not a general promotional mailing list.

How often will I get messages?

Message frequency varies. Messages are mainly conversational replies, plus occasional project continuation or check-in texts.

How do I stop?

Reply STOP. The program will send one final confirmation and then no further messages.
